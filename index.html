Admissions 2026/2027

How to Apply for Admission

Clear, step-by-step guidance for international and European applicants seeking entrance to TU Delft.

Step 01

Check Academic Prerequisites

BSc: High school diploma equivalent to Dutch VWO (e.g. IB Diploma with HL Math & Physics, A-Levels with Mathematics & Physics grade B+).
MSc: Relevant BSc degree from an accredited research university with a minimum cumulative GPA of 75% – 80% (or 3.0+ / Dutch 7.0+ scale).

Step 02

English & Standardized Tests

TOEFL iBT: Minimum total score of 100 (sub-scores 22+).
IELTS Academic: Minimum overall score of 7.0 (sub-scores 6.5+).
GRE: Required for select MSc programmes (e.g., Computer Science, Aerospace Engineering) for non-Dutch BSc holders.

Step 03

Studielink & Osiaan Portal

Submit your initial enrolment request via the official Dutch national registration portal Studielink. You will receive login details for Osiaan (TU Delft Application Portal) to upload your dossier documents.

Step 04

Evaluation & Visa / Housing

Pay the standard €100 application assessment fee. Admission results are released within 6 to 12 weeks. International students receive institutional sponsorship for Dutch residence permit (VVR) and DUWO housing allocation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for Prospective Applicants

Everything you need to know about applying, visas, housing, and life at TU Delft.

Can I work in the Netherlands during or after my studies at TU Delft? +

During studies: EU/EEA students can work without restrictions. Non-EU students with a valid Dutch student residence permit may work up to 16 hours per week (or full-time during June, July, and August) with an employer work permit (TWV).

After graduation: All TU Delft Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD graduates are eligible for the Dutch Orientation Year (Zoekjaar) residence permit. This allows you to live and work in the Netherlands for 12 months with unrestricted labor market access to find a position as a highly skilled migrant (Kennismigrant).

Is Dutch language proficiency required to apply? +

Master's Degrees: No Dutch is required. 100% of all Master of Science (MSc) programmes and PhD research at TU Delft are taught entirely in English.

Bachelor's Degrees: TU Delft offers four Bachelor's programmes taught entirely in English: Aerospace Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Applied Earth Sciences, and Nanobiology. Other Bachelor's programmes are taught in Dutch and require the NT2-II certificate.

What are the exact English proficiency requirements? +

Applicants must submit valid test results less than 2 years old:
• TOEFL iBT: Overall score of at least 100, with minimum sub-scores of 22 in each section.
• IELTS Academic: Overall score of at least 7.0, with minimum sub-scores of 6.5 in all sub-categories.
• Cambridge English: C1 Advanced or C2 Proficiency with a score of 185+.
Note: Native English speakers who completed their secondary or Bachelor's education in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, UK, or USA are exempt.

How does student accommodation in Delft work? +

TU Delft partners with DUWO, the primary student housing organization in the Netherlands, to reserve furnished student accommodation for incoming international students. Because housing in the Randstad is in high demand, students are strongly urged to complete their application and pay the confirmation fee early to secure a room in the university housing allocation pool.

What is the application evaluation fee and is it refundable? +

Applicants with an international (non-Dutch) diploma must pay a non-refundable application fee of €100 via the Osiaan portal. This covers the official credential evaluation and academic review of your dossier across your selected programme.
